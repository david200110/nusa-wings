You are a bush pilot and courier flying a little propeller plane between the
tropical islands of the Nusa archipelago. Take off from the grass strip on
Pulau Pasir, thread the target rings over the harbour town of Kota Karang,
drop medicine beside the smoking volcano of Pulau Api, and bring every
delivery home before the fuel gauge runs dry.

## How to play

- **W / S** (or Shift / Ctrl) — throttle up / down
- **Arrow keys** — pitch and roll
- **A / D** — rudder (yaw)
- **F** — flaps, **B** — brakes, **G** — landing gear
- **C / V** — switch chase view / cockpit view
- **Mouse** — look around (drag), **P** — pause, **R** — restart
- **M** — mute the engine sound

Fly six chained missions — cargo, passengers, medicine, mail, a photo run
and a rescue — each with its own goal, timer and reward. Land gently on a
runway to refuel and repair; land hard and you take damage. Stall, overspeed
and gusting sea wind all matter. Your best score is saved on your machine.

## What is inside

- A complete Three.js flight game: chase and cockpit views, a propeller
  aircraft built from primitives, lit instruments, a full HUD with altitude,
  speed, heading, throttle, fuel and a minimap.
- A procedural archipelago: a volcano island with drifting smoke, beaches,
  a harbour town with boats, two runways, animated sea, clouds and golden
  evening light.
- Original synthesised WebAudio: engine hum tied to throttle, wind, a
  touchdown thud and a mission chime. No recorded or copied audio.

Open `game/index.html` from any static server (or run `node serve.ts`)
and fly. No network needed — everything the game loads lives under `game/`.
